Stages

Wastewater treatment is a multi-stage process that cleans used water before it returns to nature. By combining physics, biology, and chemistry, modern plants transform sewage into safe water, protecting public health and local ecosystems.

1. Primary Treatment

This initial stage uses mechanical screens and large settling tanks to physically remove heavy trash, sand, and floating grease from the water.

2. Secondary Treatment

This biological stage pumps oxygen into the water to feed helpful bacteria, which rapidly consume the remaining dissolved organic waste and human impurities.

3. Tertiary Treatment

This advanced final stage filters out microscopic particles and uses ultraviolet light, chlorine, or ozone to destroy harmful viruses and bacteria before environmental release.
